How Hobart Homeowners Can Extend Water Heater Life

Routine maintenance is the difference between a water heater that lasts and one that fails early. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: an annual sediment flush, an anode rod check every three to five years, a quick relief valve test once a year, a temperature setting around 120 degrees, and annual descaling for tankless units on hard water, so small issues get caught early.

The age of your heater matters just as much. With regular maintenance, a standard tank typically lasts 8 to 12 years, a heat pump water heater 10 to 15, and a tankless unit up to 20. Once a heater passes that range and starts acting up, like rust-colored water, rumbling, or a damp spot under the tank, it's time to plan a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule is cheaper and is far less stressful than scrambling for a new heater with water spreading across the floor.

Left alone, a small problem tends to grow. Take a spent anode rod: replacing it is a short, inexpensive repair, but if nobody replaces it, the water starts eating the tank steel, and suddenly a minor fix becomes a full replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to keep our number in their phone and call at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and spares you a flooded utility room.

Common Water Heater Problems in Hobart

Most water heater failures in Hobart announce themselves before they happen. Watch for rumbling and popping during heating, rust-colored hot water, a faint rotten-egg smell, moisture around the base, or showers that run cold sooner every month. These are early signs of sediment buildup, a spent anode rod, a failing element, or corrosion starting at the bottom seam, and when they're caught early, most of them mean a modest repair or a replacement on your schedule. Ignored, they end in a failed tank and water on the floor. Pay close attention to a relief valve that drips or discharges: it's the tank telling you pressure or temperature is running too high, and it should be checked promptly.
