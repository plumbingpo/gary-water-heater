Code & Inspections

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to provide safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can lead to carbon monoxide hazards from bad venting, scalding at the tap, Legionella growth in water stored too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Gary Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas piping sizing, relief valve and discharge line routing, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where local code calls for it, and the permit and inspection process. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and health or building inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test results, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms.

Preventive Care

Scheduled Maintenance for Commercial Hot Water

A maintenance program from Gary Water Heater gets built around your equipment, how the building uses hot water, and your budget. Visits include tank flushing and anode checks, descaling of tankless heat exchangers, relief valve testing, burner, flue, and gas valve inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature readings at key fixtures. Mineral-heavy water cuts the life of commercial equipment quickly, so busy properties often benefit from quarterly visits.

Scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction of an emergency replacement and significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Businesses on a regular schedule usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and steadier water temperatures. Call us to discuss a plan designed for your property's needs and budget.
